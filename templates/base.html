Platform Features

Complete Hospital Workflow Management

The system covers the major operations required to coordinate patients, doctors, appointments, departments, and treatment records.

01

Role-Based Authentication

Dedicated access and dashboards for administrators, doctors, and patients.

02

Department Management

Create, manage, search, edit, and control hospital departments through administration.

03

Doctor Management

Manage doctor profiles, qualifications, experience, departments, and account status.

04

Availability Management

Doctors can configure their available morning and evening appointment slots.

05

Appointment Booking

Patients can select doctors, check available slots, and book appointments.

06

Appointment Tracking

Patients can track bookings while doctors manage upcoming and completed appointments.

07

Treatment Records

Doctors can add diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, and medicines after consultation.

08

Patient History

Access previous appointment and treatment information through organized patient records.

09

Search and Filtering

Locate relevant doctors, patients, departments, and hospital records efficiently.

10

Profile Management

Doctors and patients can maintain their profile and account information.

11

User Status Management

Administrators can manage account status and control access when required.

12

Centralized Records

Patient, doctor, department, appointment, and treatment data are managed through one system.
